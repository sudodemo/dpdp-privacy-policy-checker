FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is providing the company URL enough?

It can be a starting point, but the exact Privacy Policy URL is better. This static website cannot bypass CORS restrictions imposed by another website. It therefore includes a paste-text fallback.

Does the site store my policy or company URL?

No. There is no application database, account system, cookie, analytics SDK or localStorage. Assessment state exists only in the browser tab.

Does a “pass” prove DPDPA compliance?

No. This is a policy-language gap assessment. A policy can describe a control that is not actually implemented, and an operational control can exist even if the policy does not describe it.

Does the tool assess every DPDPA obligation?

It is a screening tool focused on obligations that can reasonably be evidenced from a public privacy policy. Operational testing, contracts, DPIAs, logs, security architecture and internal records need a separate audit.

How are children and SDFs handled?

The checker flags child-data and parental-consent evidence where relevant. It also flags SDF readiness, including DPIA/audit concepts. SDF duties depend on whether the entity is actually notified as a Significant Data Fiduciary.

What about the 72-hour breach requirement?

The assessment checks whether breach-response language is present. Under Rule 7, the detailed information to the Board is to be provided within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach unless a longer period is allowed by the Board.

TERMINOLOGY

Privacy jargon in plain language

Data Principal
The person the personal data is about — for example, a customer.
Data Fiduciary
The organisation deciding why and how personal data is processed.
Data Processor
A service provider that processes personal data for a Data Fiduciary.
Consent
A person's agreement to processing for a specified purpose where consent is the legal basis.
Notice
The information given to a person about what data is processed and why.
Personal data
Digital information about an identifiable individual.
Data breach
Unauthorised processing or accidental disclosure, acquisition, sharing, use, alteration, destruction or loss of personal data that compromises its confidentiality, integrity or availability.
SDF
Significant Data Fiduciary — a Data Fiduciary or class of Fiduciaries notified by the Central Government for enhanced obligations.
DPIA
Data Protection Impact Assessment — a structured assessment of significant risks to personal data and how those risks are addressed.
CORS
A browser security rule controlling whether one website can request content from another website.
