Privacy, explained simply

📱 What is personal data?

Information that relates to an identifiable person — such as your name, phone number, email or location.

🎯 Why does it matter?

Your data can reveal where you go, what you buy, who you know and how you behave. Privacy helps reduce misuse and unwanted exposure.

🛡️ What is DPDP?

India's statutory framework for digital personal data. It gives individuals rights and places obligations on organisations processing personal data.

What you can do with your privacy rights

  • 🧾 KnowAsk what personal data an organisation processes and why.
  • ✏️ CorrectAsk for inaccurate or incomplete personal data to be corrected or updated.
  • 🗑️ EraseRequest erasure where the applicable legal conditions are met, subject to lawful retention requirements.
  • 🚪 Withdraw consentWhen consent is the basis, withdrawal should be as easy as giving consent.
  • 📣 ComplainUse the organisation's grievance mechanism and applicable statutory escalation route.
  • 🚨 Respond to a breachContact the organisation, preserve evidence, secure affected accounts and follow official guidance.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is a company homepage URL enough?

An exact Privacy Policy URL is better. A homepage can be a starting point, but a static browser app cannot bypass another site's CORS restrictions. The paste-text fallback is the reliable option.

Does DataSaathi store my policy or URL?

No. There is no account, database, cookie, analytics SDK or localStorage. Assessment state is held only in memory in your browser tab.

Does “evidence found” mean the company complies?

No. It means the supplied policy contains relevant disclosure. A policy cannot prove that operational controls actually exist or work.

Why are there citizen and company modes?

Both use the same evidence approach. Citizen mode explains findings in everyday language; company mode adds legal references, risk and remediation-oriented detail.

What about the 72-hour breach requirement?

The Rules require notice to affected Data Principals without delay and provide for detailed information to the Board within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach, unless the Board permits a longer period.

TERMINOLOGY

Privacy jargon in plain language

Data Principal
The person the personal data is about — for example, a customer.
Data Fiduciary
The organisation deciding why and how personal data is processed.
Data Processor
A service provider processing personal data for a Data Fiduciary.
Consent
A person's agreement to processing for a specified purpose when consent is the legal basis.
Notice
Information given to a person about what data is processed and why.
Personal data
Digital information about an identifiable individual.
Data breach
Unauthorised processing or accidental disclosure, acquisition, sharing, use, alteration, destruction or loss that compromises personal data.
SDF
Significant Data Fiduciary — a Data Fiduciary or class notified for enhanced obligations.
DPIA
Data Protection Impact Assessment — a structured assessment of significant personal-data risks and mitigations.
CORS
A browser security mechanism controlling whether one website can request content from another.
